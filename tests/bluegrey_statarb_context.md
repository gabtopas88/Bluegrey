Project: Bluegrey / Quant-Lab

Current strategy to research:
Cross-Sectional Equity Dispersion, also called Basket StatArb.

Core hypothesis:
In the first 60 minutes of the NY session, a sector micro-universe may display residual dispersion. Some stocks overreact or underreact relative to the sector ETF and their beta. The strategy ranks residual returns at 10:00 NY, buys underperformers, shorts outperformers, builds a beta-neutral basket, and exits before the close.

Initial sector:
Semiconductors.

Initial ETF:
SMH or SOXX.

Initial universe:
30-50 liquid US-listed semiconductor stocks.

Initial signal:
Residual return from 09:30 to 10:00 adjusted by beta to sector ETF.

Entry:
10:00 NY, using next-bar open or VWAP 10:00-10:02.

Exit:
VWAP 15:53-15:55.

Constraints:
Dollar neutral.
Beta neutral.
No overnight exposure.
Max weight per name.
Realistic transaction costs.
Exclude earnings days if data is available.

Current goal:
Research only. No live trading. No order submission.
